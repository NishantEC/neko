# Neko design system, November 2026 (adopted from the owner's reference system)

Status: spec for a re-skin. Nothing here is built yet. Owner brief (2026-11-19): Neko's UI "really looks shit"; adopt the design theory and system of the owner's other product. That product is called "the reference system" below. Only values, rules and structure are recorded: no names, copy, assets or URLs.

How to read the evidence tags: `R:path:line` is a path inside the reference system's source tree, relative to its root (`R:` is never a path in this repository). `N:path:line` is a path in this repository. Values marked "computed" were derived from the reference's `color-mix()` formulas in sRGB, so they can be one unit off in a channel; the formula is given next to them and is the authority.

The web tokens stay the single source: change `web/src/themes/builtin/*.json` and `web/src/styles/*.css` first, then `apps/neko-rune/src/theme.rs` (`Hex`, `Radii`, `NekoType`) so its parser test passes again.

## 1. Principles (numbered rules)

The reference has two layers of written theory. The older one (an "editorial" voice: serif headings, mono caps eyebrows, glows) was explicitly reversed by the owner on 2026-11-13; the newer one (calm, pebble) is what ships. Neko takes the newer one only.

1. **One shape language: soft squircle pebble.** Controls 40 px tall at radius 24; sheets and dialogs 26; popovers 16. "Never hard zero-radius rail grids or cells." (`R:docs/design/settings-redesign/BRIEF.md:24-27`). Corners are continuous (`corner-shape: squircle`), circles and pills stay true arcs (`R:packages/ui/src/styles/globals.css:758-830`).
2. **No outlines anywhere.** No `border`, no `ring`, no `inset 0 0 0 1px` to separate surfaces; separate with fill steps, and use soft drop shadows only on floating things. Hairline dividers inside lists are allowed; keyboard focus rings are allowed (`BRIEF.md:28-30`).
3. **Avoid card-heavy layouts.** No boxes in boxes. A page is the surface, divided into rows by inset hairlines and whitespace; one grouped panel per section, not a stack of cards (`BRIEF.md:31-32`, `R:apps/spa/src/components/SettingsRows/settings-rows.css:1-22`).
4. **Depth from light, not chrome.** Elevation is a ladder of foreground-alpha tints (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 percent) plus a 1 px top catch-light; shadows appear only on floating layers (`R:docs/design-principles.md`, "Depth from light").
5. **Use the width.** Designed at 1600 x 1000. Fill deliberately (content column plus a side rail, wider measure for tables) instead of a narrow centred column with voids (`BRIEF.md:33-35`).
6. **One typeface, two weights of voice.** Inter 400 body, 500 labels and titles, 600 headings. "No serif, no italic accent words, no caps eyebrows, no mono caps labels." Mono only for IDs and keys (`BRIEF.md:38-39`).
7. **Pro-tool header.** A plain 20/600 title, one muted meta line, actions on the right (`BRIEF.md:41`).
8. **Icons, never emoji.** One line-icon set (regular weight; the filled weight marks the active nav item), slightly dimmed to about 46 percent foreground (`BRIEF.md:36-37`, `R:apps/spa/src/components/UnifiedAppSidebar/UnifiedAppSidebar.parts.tsx:88-89`).
9. **Calm navigation.** Monochrome, dimmed icons; active row is a gentle fill step with full-contrast text, "no raised pill, no shadow"; no counts on nav rows, no shortcut chips on rows, spacing groups instead of dividers, one tiny group label at most (`R:docs/design/sidebar-redesign/SPEC.md:19-23`).
10. **Colour is state, at low alpha.** Neutral chrome; status appears as a 6 px dot or a 14 percent tint pill with text beside it. "Never saturated fills." The primary action is ink (near black in light, near white in dark), not a hue (`R:docs/design-principles.md`, tone rule; `R:apps/spa/src/components/Sky/sky-materials.css:142-157`).
11. **Dark is graphite, not navy.** Neutral greys with no blue cast (`BRIEF.md:43-44`).
12. **No backdrop blur inside scrolling regions.** It renders holes in some browsers. Glass is for the sidebar panel, the composer and floating layers only (`BRIEF.md:45`).
13. **Motion is short, caused and quiet.** 150 / 220 / 320 ms on one ease-out curve, popups scale from 0.97 with a 4 px drift, nothing loops except a "working" indicator, everything collapses under reduced motion (section 6).
14. **Copy is short, human, sentence case.** "People", not users; no "Manage your...", no "seamlessly", no research jargon (`BRIEF.md:40`). Neko's own calm-copy rules (DESIGN.md section 6) already agree.
15. **Numbers are tabular, units quiet.** Counts and times use tabular numerals in the muted grey; IDs and keys use the mono face (`R:docs/design-principles.md`, "Data as instruments", mono part only).

| popover and menu | 16 | |

Rules: a surface nested in another steps down by the inset ("outer minus inset"). A token must stay under about 0.4 x the height of the shortest control using it, or the squircle collapses to a circle (`R:packages/ui/src/styles/globals.css:263-292`). A wrapper that hugs a rounded child uses the child's radius exactly (`R:packages/ui/src/styles/globals.css:824+`).

## 8. Visual character

The reference reads as a calm, soft instrument. In light it is a white, almost paperlike content panel with generously rounded corners floating on a pale cool-grey chrome, beside a tall frosted sidebar with a faint blue tint; in dark it is true graphite, not navy, with the same two-panel structure. Everything touchable is a pebble: pill-radiused controls that are slightly lit on their top edge, with no outlines at all, and inputs are shallow wells pressed into the surface. Settings are not cards but one grouped slab per section, divided by hairlines that stop short of the edges, with a quiet 20 px heading above and rows that are roomy (66 px) rather than dense. Type is a single clean sans at medium weight with confident, slightly tight headings; colour is almost absent, and what remains is a 6 px green or amber dot or a faint tinted pill beside words. The home screen is the only place that gets atmosphere (a dotted day-and-night sky, a glass composer, round chips); inside the product the mood is quiet, tactile and unhurried, with motion that is short, soft and only ever the answer to a click.
